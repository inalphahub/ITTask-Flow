WHY IT TASKFLOW

Work should feel connected, not scattered.

IT teams coordinate projects, assign responsibilities, track progress, and work across different roles every day. When that work lacks a shared structure, maintaining clarity becomes harder.

IT TaskFlow is being developed around a straightforward idea: give teams a more organized place to manage their work, understand their responsibilities, and follow project progress.

Our focus is on making everyday workflows easier to understand, while keeping the work relevant to each person's role and project.

A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Questions about IT TaskFlow?

A few details about what we are building and how the product is intended to work.

Explore documentation

IT TaskFlow is a project and task management workspace being developed to help IT teams organize assignments, coordinate project work, and maintain visibility into responsibilities.

The product direction focuses on software organizations and the people involved in their project workflows, including employees, team leads, project managers, and organizational administrators.

The intended experience connects tasks to their projects and presents relevant information according to a user's assignments and permitted access, rather than treating all project work as interchangeable.
